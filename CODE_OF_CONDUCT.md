# Code of Conduct

neotools is a small project that wants to be a friendly place to ask questions, report bugs, translate and contribute code. This
code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the community:

- being kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project, to Swift or to the command line;
- respecting different opinions and experiences;
- giving and accepting feedback about the work, not the person;
- owning mistakes, and learning from them.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind;
- trolling, insults, personal or political attacks;
- harassment in public or in private;
- publishing someone else's private information without their permission;
- anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, email about the project) and when someone
represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for explaining and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues
and other contributions that do not follow them, and warn or ban a person for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or
harmful.

Report a problem to the maintainer at **reachdevel@gmail.com**. Every report is read, kept private and answered. The maintainer
will not share who made it.

Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending
on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.
