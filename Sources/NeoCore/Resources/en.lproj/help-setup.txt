{program} - type du, get neodu

usage: {program} [--install | --uninstall | --status]

Without options, a few pages ask which shells and which commands.

  --install         every command in every shell, no questions
  --uninstall       take everything out again
  --status          what is set up now
  --help --version

Examples:
  {program}
  {program} --install
  {program} --status
